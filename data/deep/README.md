# data/deep

The data of the ias route (see [docs/deep.md](../../docs/deep.md)): the traces of four families, the witnesses of their points, the re-closed stretches, the table of the 19 traces started with the route (the group below), run or not run, and the numbers that the tests of `npm run test:ias` compare with. About 4.2 MB in all. The files are under CC BY 4.0.

## Traces, witnesses, re-closed stretches

The four families are t35 -, t44 -, t62 - and t72 - (the label `t35m` is the family t35 followed in the direction of falling lam; `m` and `p` are the two directions). Every file is compact json written by `tools/family-follow-ias.mjs` and `tools/witness.py` after they were reduced to the fields below; the complete outputs are not stored here.

- `traces/<label>.json`: the points of the trace (loop step, lam, u1, u2, t, T*, L*, theta, turns, closest approach, closure), the start, the direction, the ratio of guard G1b, the guard counts, why the trace stopped and the accepted steps that could not be recorded (`unrecorded`, with their start values). 481 kB (t35m, 1690 points), 358 kB (t44m), 106 kB (t62m), 92 kB (t72m). The accepted steps in `unrecorded` have T* and L* from their start values (`Tstar`, `Lstar`, added to the files afterwards with `perpEL` of `src/perp.js`, no rerun), but no `minDist` and no closure (see docs/deep.md). The folder holds the compact files of 14 traces: the four above and the other ten runs that have an output (t12 +, t14 +, t28 +, t35 +, t72 +, t28 -, t44 +, t62 +, t81 -, t81 +), in the same format; t12 - (one point, 5 s) has no trace output. `traces/earlier/` has the outputs of the earlier traces of t35 -, t44 -, t62 -, t72 - whose last points are the starts of the four traces (the start is that point closed again with 24 pieces: lam equal, u1, u2 and t differ by 1.5e-10 or less; the format of the driver of that time, only the field `label` was changed to the name of the family).
- `witnesses/<label>.json`: for every witnessed point (`<label>-k<index>` for a recorded point, `<label>-u<step>` for an unrecorded step) the start values and the results of `tools/witness.py` for the presets w1 and w1b: smallest pair distance (`dmin_parabolic`), closure of the full period, relative change of the energy and of the angular momentum, seconds. w1b is there where the w1 minimum was below 1e-7 and for all 21 unrecorded steps of t35 - (12 of them, steps 2, 4 to 6, 16 to 22 and 24, have a w1 minimum between 1e-7 and 5.5e-7; the minimum of a point is taken from w1 where it is 1e-7 or more and from w1b below that, `reclose/*.json` stores the w1b values). The fields `s` (the s of the rule that keeps a recorded point: its lam differs by at least 0.01 s from that of the last kept point) and `added` (ids that the rule does not give) say which points were selected. `python tools/trace-witnesses.py reading data/deep/witnesses/<label>.json [--trace data/deep/traces/<label>.json]` gives the reading (strict depth, turns) from this file alone and, with the trace, how many recorded points were not selected. 29 kB, 46 kB, 119 kB, 115 kB.
- `reclose/<label>.json`: the accepted steps (and the midpoints between consecutive steps) that were closed again at the same lam by `tools/reclose.py`: start values, re-closed values, number of updates, residual, whether it is the same orbit, and for the midpoints the w1b witness at the re-closed values. t35m: 25 steps and 24 midpoints; the others: 3 steps and 2 midpoints each; all 64 converged. 31 kB, 4 kB each.
- `traces-table.json` and `traces-table.csv`: the 19 traces of the group, 15 run and 4 not run (the start orbit trips guard G2), with the start values, direction, how the start was found, why the trace stopped, the number of points, the run time in seconds, the ratio K of guard G1b of the code that ran the trace (`guard_ratio`; 400 for the two traces of batch 0, 390 for the others, empty if not run), the strict witnessed depth, the turn and the further turns, and the number of points whose closure failed. The group is these 19 traces (family and direction in lam): t12 +, t35 -, a14 +, t12 -, t14 +, t19 -, t28 +, t35 +, t44 -, t62 -, t72 -, t72 +, t18 -, t18 +, t28 -, t44 +, t62 +, t81 -, t81 +; a label tNN is row NN of `data/orbit-table.json` for the traces of batch 3, whose `start_row` says so. The column `batch` groups the traces by how they were selected: batch 0 the two earlier traces run again (t12 + and t35 -), batch 1 the first three traces and the starts that needed a looser start test, batches 2 and 3 the further traces. The column `c0` says whether the first steps of the trace continue the stored curve (the earlier trace that gave the start, `valid`), or only move lam in the stored direction because the start is a single stored orbit (`valid (direction only)`), or neither (`not valid`; the trace then has no strict depth). `start_source` says where the start values come from; of the earlier traces named there only those of t35 -, t44 -, t62 - and t72 - are in the repository (`traces/earlier/`). In the json the rows of the last group also name the row of `data/orbit-table.json` that the start comes from (`start_file`, `start_row`, counted from 0; the start in the table is that row closed again by the code at 24 pieces, `start_note` gives the largest change). 18 kB and 4 kB.

The complete outputs behind these files (the full results of `tools/witness.py` and `tools/reclose.py` (also for all 854 witnessed points), including the end states, the full outputs and per-attempt records of the 14 traces that have them, the iterates of the piece scan, and the matrices, inputs and runs behind the files of `extra/` that came from later computations: 4149 files, 42 MB) are deposited on Zenodo [DOI at release] and are not in the repository; its README.txt says what each folder is and its FILES.txt has the size and sha256 of every file.

The traces were run with a copy of `src/ias/reg.js` whose sha256 is stored in the field `frozen` of the full trace outputs and of `traces/earlier/t35m.json` (`reg_js_sha256` 5bd3ed813f733eec9371e824d629cfe0483edbe9c0a7ec39f09e0ae7115ea612, and `settings_sha256` 698218875dfc3b0bf2fb1a3615dc308974bede69aa60180d849dcf34824c9d1a, which is the sha256 of the released `src/ias/reg-settings.json`). The released `reg.js` has another sha256. A comparison line by line, with line endings ignored and the text before `//` taken as the code of the line, found no difference in the code of any line between that copy and the file as it was committed for the route: they differ in comments only. Afterwards the functions `opTest`, `singleOp` and `chainRoundTrip`, which no test, tool or run called, an unused local function of the energy constant, and the `export` of the names that no other file imports were removed from the released file, so the code that runs is the same. The replay test (`test/replay.test.js`: on the machine of the stored run the first 31 accepted steps of t35 - are bit-identical with the stored trace) and the sample run (`tools/deep-sample.mjs`) show that the released code follows the stored path.

The start of a trace. The field `start` of a trace file is the start after the first thing that `tools/family-follow-ias.mjs` does with the values it is given: it closes them again with 24 pieces (`closePerpMS`) and follows the family from the result. The original runs were given other values, the command-line starts, which `extra/start-shift.json` lists for all 14 traces (the closing of a command-line start gives the stored start bit for bit in every case). Given the command-line start, the driver reproduces the stored accepted steps bit for bit on the machine of the stored runs: t72 - (all 134 accepted steps of the first 130 recorded points), t62 - (23 steps), t72 + and t81 - (20 steps each), and t35 - from its replay start (31 steps). Given the stored start, the driver closes it a second time. Where that moves the start (t44 +, t62 +, t72 -, t72 +, t81 - and t81 +, by 4.3e-13 to 4.7e-12 relative in the largest of u1, u2, t) the run differs from the stored trace from its first accepted step on (t72 +: 3.1e-10 over 20 steps; t81 -: 3.1e-12 over 20 steps; t72 -: 1.3e-10 in the first accepted step, 1.2e-4 after 134 steps), and where it does not move it (the other eight traces; run: t35 -, t44 -, t62 -) the run is bit-identical. The copy of the code that ran the traces gives the same accepted steps as the released code, bit for bit (20 steps of t72 -), from the stored start (both differ from the stored trace) and from the command-line start (both equal it). As far as can be told the second closing moves the start because the nodes it starts from are integrated again from (u1, u2, t) and their continuity residual is above the corrector tolerance for the orbits with the closest encounters. So to reproduce a stored trace the driver is given the command-line start of `extra/start-shift.json`, not the stored start (the replay of t35 - above is not affected: its start is not moved).

## For the tests and the sample run

- `witness-acceptance.json`: the multiple precision flow of `tools/refine-mp.py` (50 digits, tolerance 1e-30, step floor 1e-20) against the exact Kepler flow of `tools/kepler-ref.py`,
  one period of a near-radial pair with periapsis distance q = 1e-7 to 1e-10. `tools/witness-acceptance.py` reruns it and compares (`test/witness-acceptance.test.js`).
- `gate-a-subset.json`: seven rows of the test of `src/ias/reg.js` against the exact Kepler flow of the pair: masses, start state, interval, the exact end state as a double-double
  (`ref_hi` + `ref_lo`), the sensitivity S and the floor of the limit, and the stored end state and error (`test/gate-a.test.js`).
- `gate-a-all.json`: the 80 rows of `tools/gate-a-rows.py` with the error, limit, zone and end state of the stored run (`node tools/gate-a.mjs rows.json --stored data/deep/gate-a-all.json`).
- `reclose-t35m-gap.json`: accepted steps 9, 10 and 11 of a continuation of the family t35 - (start values), the same orbits re-closed at the same lam by `tools/reclose.py`, and the witness minimum
  of the pair distance and the closure of the start values from `tools/witness.py --preset w1b` (`test/reclose.test.js`).
- `t35m-replay.json`: the start of that continuation, its first 10 recorded points on the machine of the stored run and the loop steps of the accepted steps that were not recorded there; `test/replay.test.js` takes the start from here and compares the accepted steps of the continuation (lam, u1, u2, t) with `traces/t35m.json` to 1e-9 relative, and `node tools/deep-sample.mjs` takes its start from here.
- `henon-t35m.json`: the stored median and largest deviation from Hénon's relation dT* = pi Ls d(turns) with the signed Ls = L |E|^(1/2) (for t35 -, where L < 0, dT* = -pi L* d(turns)) between consecutive points of `traces/t35m.json` (`test/henon.test.js`).
- `sample/expected-summary.txt`: the summary that `node tools/deep-sample.mjs` has to print; `sample/output.txt` is the whole output of the stored run with the times (the name of its temporary folder is replaced by `<temp folder>`).

The numbers were computed on Windows 11 with Node v24.15.0, Python 3.14.6, REBOUND 5.2.2 and mpmath 1.4.1. On that machine the end states of `src/ias/reg.js` and the replayed accepted steps are equal to
the stored ones bit for bit; on another platform the end states can differ in the last digits, so the tests with exact equality also accept small relative differences where the file says so.

## Extra files with the numbers behind the text

Compact copies of results that the documents use and that are not behind a test (`data/deep/extra/`, 655 kB, each file has a `what` field). The ids in these files follow the scheme of the file they were taken from: `k<index>` and `reg-t35m_<index>` are kept points of an earlier trace of t35 - that is not stored; `T-<label>_<index>` is the point with that position in the trace file of the label, and `T-<label>-unrec_<step>` an unrecorded step (`closure-failed.json`; the step of the point is in the field `step`); `T0c-unrec_<step>` is the unrecorded accepted step of that number of the trace of t35 - in `traces/t35m.json` (`w1c-gap.json`), and `w1c` there is a run of the flow with 60 digits and tolerance 1e-36, which is not a preset of `tools/witness.py`.

- `extra/piece-scan.json`: the scan of 9016 pieces of the multiple shooting against the multiple precision flow (counts by group and by the smallest pair minimum of the piece, the 18 pieces with an error of 1e-12 or more, the 69 kept points that were scanned) and the one event with an error far above the corrector tolerance: piece 18 of kept point 11 of the trace of t35 -, an unflagged IAS15 piece after the encounter piece, 1.18e-7, with the w1 witness of that point (`point_11_witness`, closure 4.125e-6, rejected). 7360 of the 9016 scanned pieces come from 320 iterates of earlier runs that are not stored here, so the scan cannot be rerun from the repository.
- `extra/gate-a-extension.json`: 44 rows of the test of `src/ias/reg.js` against a 40 digit flow (periapsis distance q = 1e-4 to 1e-3, third body at R, ratio R/measured from 50 to 3333), all within the limit, and the value K = 390 of guard G1b derived from them.
- `extra/closure-failed.json`: the 15 stored points of t28 - and t81 + whose witnessed closure is 1e-6 or more, refined at the same lam in multiple precision (stored values, refined values, change, the witness of the rounded refined orbit). The multipliers of these points (the eigenvalues of the monodromy matrix, computed with `tools/point-diagnostic.py`) are not given: they failed their quality checks.
- `extra/old-variants.json`: seven earlier runs of the Dormand-Prince code of version 0.2.33 (t19 -, t35 +, t35 - with the corrector tolerance 1e-11; the same and a14 + with 1e-10): where each lost the family (the closest approach of the tool at the last point, `end_d`, a tool value that is not witnessed) and the end of its reliable stretch as read then (`endx`). The numbers in the first section of `docs/deep.md` are the range of `end_d`; the tighter integrator tolerances belong to the run of a14 + only.
- `extra/reg-matrix-pieces.json`: the start states and tau of the six encounter pieces of t35 - steps 1 and 10 and t44 - step 34 for `test/reg-matrix.test.js` (the 12 x 12 matrix of `reg.js` against central differences, and symplecticity).
- `extra/reg-unequal-mass.json`: one row of the test of `src/ias/reg.js` with the masses 1 and 0.1 (a = 1, e = 0.9, one period) and the exact end state from `tools/kepler-ref.py` with mu = m1 + m2, made by `tools/gate-a-unequal.py`; `test/reg-unequal.test.js` reads it.
- `extra/gate-a-deep-q.json`: eight more rows of the test of `src/ias/reg.js` against the exact Kepler flow (type A2 of `tools/gate-a-rows.py`, periapsis distance q = 1e-10 and 1e-11, one and five periods, two start phases) with the error, limit, zone and end state; 7 within the limit, one HARD FAIL (q = 1e-11, from the apocentre, five periods: error 1.8e-11, limit 5e-13). `node tools/gate-a.mjs data/deep/extra/gate-a-deep-q.json` reads it.
- `extra/w1c-gap.json`: the witness minimum at accepted steps 9, 10 and 11 of t35 - with the 50 digit flow and with a 60 digit flow (relative differences 6.6e-6, 3.7e-5, 7.5e-8).
- `extra/w1-acceptance.json`: the 40 digit flow of the preset w1 (tolerance 1e-26, step floor 1e-14) against the exact Kepler flow of a near-radial pair at the periapsis distances q = 1e-6, 1e-7 and, as information, 3e-8 (errors 8.7e-21, 4.7e-19, 3.3e-18; limit 1e-17); the command of each row is in the file.
- `extra/stall-svd.json`: the singular values of the extended Jacobian [J | dF/dlam] (279 x 280, 24 pieces) of the multiple shooting system at ten stored accepted steps of t35 - (steps 1, 100, 142, 165, 700, 2190) and t44 - (50, 588, 1225, 1588), after closing each at its lam with the ias route: the smallest, second and third smallest and largest of the whole matrix, the two smallest of J alone, the closing residual. Made by `tools/stall-matrix.mjs` (the matrices) and `tools/stall-svd.py`; the matrices are in the raw files. 5 kB.
- `extra/stall-jacobian-mp.json`: the 3 x 4 Jacobian d(r1, r2, r3)/d(u1, u2, t, lam) of the closing conditions of `tools/reclose.py` (50 digits) at six stored points (t35 - steps 165, 700, 1227, 2190; t44 - steps 588, 1588): singular values, sigma3/sigma1, lam_lim from Aitken's delta-squared on the stored lam, the distance |lam - lam_lim|. Made by `tools/stall-jacobian-mp.py` (prepare, reclose, jacobian); the jobs and the results of the re-closing are in the raw files. 5 kB.
- `extra/multipliers-t44m.json`: the multipliers of the orbit at the stored values of t44 - at the step 1588 (40 digits, `tools/point-diagnostic.py --mult-only`, 1056 s): four real non-trivial multipliers -6.4e-4, -1.29e-2, -77.6, -1556.9 and the two quality checks of the tool. 2 kB.
- `extra/reclose-w1b.json`: `tools/reclose.py` at the stored start values of the 33 witnessed points that have a w1b result, at their stored lam: converged, same orbit, updates, first residual, the relative change of u1, u2 and t, and the comparison with `reclose/*.json` for the points that are accepted steps of both. Made by `tools/reclose-w1b.py`; the results of each point are in the raw files. 15 kB.
- `extra/reclose-all.json`: the same for all 854 witnessed points of the four traces (t35 - 69, t44 - 124, t62 - 336, t72 - 325) at their stored lam: for each point converged, same orbit, updates, first residual, largest relative change of u1, u2 and t, seconds; totals and the largest values per trace. All 854 converged in one update to the same orbit; the largest relative change of a point is 8.2e-11 (t44 -) and the largest first residual 6.7e-10. Made by `tools/reclose-all.py` (`queue DIR`, `tools/run-queue.py DIR/queue.json 3`, `summary DIR OUT.json`); the output of `tools/reclose.py` of each point is in the raw files. 150 kB.
- `extra/pericentres.json`: the osculating two-body pericentre at the closest sample of the 50 digit flow at six points (the smallest witnessed value of t35 -, t44 -, t62 -, t72 - and the re-closed midpoints 10-11 of t35 - and 34-35 of t44 -) next to the stored parabolic estimate. Made by `tools/pericentres.py`. 3 kB.
- `extra/db82-nearest.json`: the 130 converted rows of Davoust and Broucke (1982) against the four stored stretches (as the orbit and as an n fold repeat, T*, L* and turns modulo 1, both signs): matches (none), near rows, the five nearest rows of each family, rows in the box of the stretch. Made by `tools/db82-families.py`. 4 kB.

- `extra/first-step-piece18.json`, `extra/piece18-states.json`: IAS15 over piece 18 of the kept points k10, k11, k12 of t35 - for adaptive_mode 0 to 3 and the first steps 0.001, 1e-4, 1e-6 and 'timescale', against `reg.js` (`tools/first-step-check.py piece18`), and the start states and `reg.js` end states of the three pieces (used by `test/ias-first-step.test.js`). `extra/first-step-scan.json`: IAS15 on the unflagged pieces of the 320 stored iterates of the scan with the first steps 'legacy' and 'timescale' against `reg.js` (`tools/first-step-check.py scan`). `extra/first-step-runs.json`: short runs of the driver with both first steps against the stored traces (cross-check counts, differences).
- `extra/reclose-beyond-stall.json`: the creeping traces closed again beyond their last lam (`tools/reclose-beyond.py`) and the w1 witnesses of the orbits at lam_last - 1e-4. `extra/creep-attempts.json`: the corrector during the creep (`tools/creep-attempts.py`; the creep rule is in [docs/deep.md](../../docs/deep.md)). `extra/stall-jacobian-3x3.json`: sigma of the 3 x 3 block of the closing Jacobian at the six stall points (`tools/stall-jacobian-3x3.py`). `extra/stall-remedy-t44m.json`: the three resumed runs of t44 -.
- `extra/dp45-same-start.json`: the default (Dormand-Prince) route from the start of t35 - and from the start of its earlier trace, and its closing residuals at the 22 points of the earlier trace.
- `extra/dp45-same-start-all.json`: the same comparison for t35 -, t44 -, t62 - and t72 -: the default (Dormand-Prince) route from the command-line start and from the start of the earlier trace of each (15 runs; the two t35 - rows with 24 pieces are copied from `dp45-same-start.json`; every start was run with 24 pieces and, except the t35 - replay start, which no number of pieces closes, again without the pieces argument, where the tool takes the first of 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64 pieces that closes the start: 8 for t35 - and t44 - from the earlier trace and for t44 - from the command line, 16 for t62 - from the earlier trace and for t72 - from both starts, 32 for t62 - from the command line): whether the start is moved onto the family, the accepted steps (none in any of the 15 runs), where and why each run stops, and for the starts that could not be moved with 24 pieces the closing residuals with 8 to 64 pieces. 31 kB.
- `extra/reclose-midpoints-deep.json`: `tools/reclose.py` on the midpoints between consecutive witnessed points within 5 points on each side of the deepest witnessed point of t44 - (step 34), t62 - (step 9) and t72 - (step 97), 30 in all, each witnessed with `tools/witness.py` (w1, and w1b where the w1 minimum is below 1e-7): updates, residual, re-closed values, change against the start values, the witnessed minimum and closure, and the comparison with the six midpoints that are also in `reclose/*.json` (identical re-closed values). 42 kB.
- `extra/kepler-stm.json`: the state transition matrix of the exact Kepler flow of the equal-mass pair in 60 digits (`tools/kepler-stm.py`), for `test/kepler-stm.test.js`. `extra/mind1-bias.json`: `minD1` of `reg.js` divided by the exact pericentre distance (`tools/mind1-check.mjs`).

- `extra/start-shift.json`: for the 14 traces of `traces/`, the start the original run was given (command-line start), the stored start, the relative change of the closing in both directions, and the first accepted steps of new runs of the driver against the stored traces (first step 'legacy'). Made by running `closePerpMS` and `tools/family-follow-ias.mjs` from both starts.

- `extra/orbit-track-t35m-step10.json`: the positions of the three bodies over one period (723 samples, dense around the passage of the close pair) from the stored start values of the accepted step 10 of t35 -, made by `tools/orbit-track.mjs` with `src/ias/reg.js` only; the picture of the orbit in the paper is drawn from it.

Each of these files has a "what" field and the command; the values are the stored output of that command. The tools took the stored files as input and gave the same values again for the files that were run again (the singular values of one step, one re-closed point, one pericentre, all six Jacobians, the comparison with the rows of Davoust and Broucke); the long runs (the multipliers, the whole re-closing of the 33 and of the 854 points) were not repeated.
