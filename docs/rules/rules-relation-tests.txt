Rules for the tests of the relations of the zeros of the upper branch of the R satellites (k = 1, n = 5 to 36), copied from the local rules file. They were written on 2026-10-11 before the numbers of these tests were computed; the script is tools/satellite-tests.mjs (it reads the data files of this repository). The numbers that existed before (tools/satellite-relations.mjs, version 0.2.22) are not changed by these tests. The times in the deviations are the clock of the machine.

DATA. T*, L* of the zero of every n: the 30 digit orbits of data/satellite-candidates.json for n = 7 and 9 to 36 (so the zeros of n = 24, 26, 27, 28 are the refined ones), the stored zero of
data/branch-points/<n>-upper.json for n = 5, 6, 8 (rotation left below 1e-11 turns there). n T*_R(n): the T* of the n fold repeat at the branch point (first point of the satellite curve), which
is the same as n times the T* of the member of the R family with the upper nu = 1/n of the stored trace to the precision of the interpolation (checked in the script and reported).

WHAT WAS WRITTEN DOWN BEFORE THE RUN. Only predictor B (docs/rules/rules-n21-up.txt: T*_n = n T*_R(n) - (a + b/n), fitted on n = 8 to 20; the multiplier trends also, not tested here). The L* fit
(a + b/n + c/n^2) and the sqrt(n) form of T* were found after the zeros for n >= 21 were known, so no result on them below is out of sample in the sense of a prediction. This is said where the results are given (docs/r-family-satellites.md).

TEST 3. Robustness of the k = 1 relations (B, the sqrt(n) form a n + b + c sqrt(n) for T*, and the L* fit a + b/n + c/n^2).
 (3a) Leave-one-out over n = 8 to 36: for each n0 the relation is fitted on the other n of 8 to 36 and evaluated at n0. Reported: the largest and the rms error, the ranges of the coefficients, and for B
      the range of the prediction at n0 compared with the fit on all 29 points. (This is interpolation and is said to be.)
 (3b) Trailing windows: fit on n = 8 to m for m = 14 to 35, predict n = m + 1 to 36. Reported: the largest error for each m, the error at n = 36, the coefficients, for L* also the limit a.
 (3c) Different windows: fits on [lo, hi] for lo in {5, 8, 10, 12}, hi in {20, 24, 28, 36}, evaluated on the points outside the window (reported where there are some) and on n = 36.
 WHAT COUNTS AS HOLDING: for the T* relations (B and sqrt(n)) the largest error in (3a) and in every (3b) with m >= 14 is at most 0.005 (the largest error so far is 0.0031); for the L* fit it is at most
 1e-3 (so far 5.1e-4). The coefficients are called stable if the prediction at n = 36 (T* or L*) moves by less than the same bound between all the fits of (3a) to (3c) that include n = 36 in the fit,
 and by less than twice the bound in the fits that do not. If a relation fails the bound it is reported with the case that breaks it. The bound is fixed here and not changed after the numbers are seen.

TEST 5. The heuristic for the correction of B (suggested after the data were seen; a first-order heuristic, not a derivation): at the branch point the n fold repeat has
 rotation n |theta_R| = 1 + eps_n and delta_n = C eps_n, C about 8, C = |dT*/dtheta| along the satellite curve near the branch point.
 Computed from data/branch-points/<n>-upper.json for n = 5 to 36 (k = 1): eps_n = n |member.turns| - 1; the rotation of the first point of the satellite curve (checked: equal to -eps_n); delta_n = T* of the
 first point of the curve minus T* of the zero (the refined zero where there is one); the ratio delta_n / eps_n; the local slope C_loc = |dT*/dturns| between the first point and the first point whose turns
 differs by more than 1e-7 (the slope near the branch point); the mean slope along the curve is the same number as the ratio (an identity, said where the results are given). Also the position of the maximum of eps_n and of delta_n.
 For k > 1: every file data/branch-points/<k>-<n>-<branch>-<theta>.json with a zero (stored zero; its rotation left is at most 3e-8 turns, checked), eps = the rotation of the first point of the curve
 (the nearest whole number to n |member.turns| removed), delta = T* of the first point minus T* of the zero, ratio |delta| / |eps|, the closure tier of the zero from data/branch-summary.json.
 WHAT COUNTS AS HOLDING: the ratio |delta| / |eps| is between 7 and 9 (that is C = 8 within about 12 %). For k = 1 the report gives the n for which it holds and the n where it does not; the local slope C_loc is
 said to hold where it is within 12 % of the ratio. For k > 1 the files with a ratio outside 7 to 9 are listed as failures (four are expected from a first look at the data). A hold in this sense says that the correction has the size
 and the shape of eps; it is not evidence for a derivation, and C is not predicted independently of the curves.

TEST 4 (reduced: the Henon family and the A family are not traced). For the zeros of n = 5 to 36: whether the satellites follow (i) the relation of the PRL type, the satellite lies on the
 same curve of T*/k against L* as the parent family (T*_zero / n = T*_R(L*_zero), the R family at the L* of the zero, from the stored trace data/r-family-trace.json, all crossings of L* listed), or (ii) the
 relation with matching nu, T*_zero / n = T*(member with nu = 1/n) - delta_n / n, with L*_zero and L*_member far apart. Reported: for every n the T*/n of the zero, the T* of the R curve at the L* of the zero
 (all crossings), the T*/n and L* of the member, the relative differences (i) and (ii). WHAT COUNTS: (i) is said to hold for an n if the relative difference to the nearest crossing is below 1e-3 (the size of
 delta_n / (n T*_R)), and (ii) holds for the n where the relative difference is below 1e-3. The statement about the PRL curve is only about the R family; the BHH family (Broucke A1, Henon orbit 11) is not
 traced here and nothing is said about the CPC satellites. This is not a reproduction of the result of Jankovic et al.


DEVIATIONS (declared before the first run, 2026-10-11 10:20): (1) in test 3 the relation B is evaluated with n T*_R(n) from the interpolated crossing of nu = 1/n on the stored trace, as published in
docs/r-family-satellites.md and tools/satellite-relations.mjs (not with the first point of the satellite curve, which the DATA paragraph above says); the two differ by up to 2.4e-3 (measured; 7e-4 was guessed when this was written) and the difference is
reported; test 5 uses the first point of the curve, as written. (2) For k > 1 the closure tier is matched to the file by k, n, branch and the theta of the file name (4 digits).
DEVIATION 3 (2026-10-11, after the first run, declared here): the matching of the closure tier of a k > 1 file to data/branch-summary.json by the theta of the file name failed for 8 of the 17 files (the
name has the theta that was asked for, rounded to 4 digits, not the theta of the member, which differs by up to 9e-4). The match is now by k, n, branch and the theta of the member inside the file
(|e.theta - |member.turns|| < 1e-9). Nothing else was changed; the numbers of the other tests did not depend on it.
