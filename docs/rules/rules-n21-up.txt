SATELLITES OF THE R FAMILY, k = 1, UPPER BRANCH, n = 21 UPWARD. LOCAL ONLY (a local folder), nothing in the repo, nothing pushed or sent. Written 2026-10-10 before the first run; nothing below may change after the run starts; a change that cannot be avoided goes into DEVIATIONS with what and why.

TIME. 4 hours wall-clock from the start time written at the end; at the deadline everything running is stopped and whatever is done is reported.

WHAT IS RUN. Upper branches only (the lower branches of n = 5 to 20 never reached a rotation 0). For n = 21, 22, ... the member of the R family with nu = 1/n on the upper nu (crossing found in data/r-family-trace.json by the rule of tools/nu-map.mjs, extended in n; the theta of each is given to the tool), then branch-point-n.mjs (a copy of tools/branch-point.mjs in which only the paths differ) with --control. The jobs are started in the order of n, 10 at a time.

LIMITS AND TOLERANCES, UNCHANGED from n <= 20: member by bisection |nu - 1/n| < 1e-11, residual < 1e-11 (8 pieces); the n fold repeat with 8, 16, 24, 32 or 40 pieces, residual < 1e-9; quadratic bifurcation equation h = 2e-3, 36 directions, two real roots; pseudo arclength, tangent turning < 0.35 rad per step, step <= 0.03 and <= 6 % of the distance from the branch point, 700 accepted steps at most, stop at a closest approach below 0.03, zero of theta |theta| < 1e-11; both senses; control = the repeat direction. Every stop is a limit of the method, not a result.

EVERY ZERO WITH ROTATION 0: refined to 30 digits (tools/refine-mp.py; the closure of the full period measured on the rounded stored values), word, alignments, repeat test (n up to 200), double precision closure tier, largest nontrivial multiplier and stability (src/relative.js), REBOUND 5.2.2 (IAS15, default settings: closure from the double values and the multipliers), and the comparison with the atlas (catalogue.json of 2026-10-09), the 99 CPC satellites, my 102, the 9 periodic rows of Davoust and Broucke, Henon 1976 and the 18 rows of Li et al.: hit = T* and L* within 1e-5 with repeats up to 12 either way and the same alignments, near = 5e-3, every pair at the same period decided by the start values (src/identity.js, the same orbit when they agree to 1e-6).

STOP RULE (applied in the order of n; results of larger n that are already running or finished are kept and reported but not used for the rule). Stop when (a) 3 consecutive n are undecided (an n is undecided when neither sense of the upper branch reaches a rotation 0), or (b) the closure tier of a zero drops below reliable (weak or failed), or (c) the crossings of the stored R trace run out. About (c): the stored upper nu of data/r-family-trace.json decrease along the family to a minimum 0.017034 (1/58.7) and then stay (the last 40 steps converge to one point, T* 1.880, theta 0.0171); the upper nu crosses 1/n for n up to 58. So the crossings run out after n = 58, and a longer trace of the same family would not give more unless the family continues beyond the point where the stored trace ends (not tested). The jobs are n = 21 to 58.

PREDICTIONS (fitted only on n = 5 to 20, data that exist before the run).
 Predictor B: T*(n) = n T*_R(n) - delta(n), n T*_R(n) from the trace crossing (interpolated between two steps), delta(n) = -0.0033 + 0.4633/n (least squares on n = 8 to 20; rms residual 8.2e-4; the form a + b/n was chosen over a + b n, rms 2.2e-3, because a linear delta would become negative). The prediction holds for an n when the T* of the zero is within 0.05 of it. For comparison, a + b n (a = 0.0711, b = -0.00276) is in the table.
 Largest nontrivial multiplier: the trend of n = 13 to 20 (1.712, 2.609, 3.426, 4.235, 5.054, 5.890, 6.745, 7.621; increments 0.90, 0.82, 0.81, 0.82, 0.84, 0.86, 0.88): linear, 7.621 + 0.85 (n - 20) (the primary prediction), and a quadratic fit (4.6525 + 0.83637 x + 0.00172 x^2, x = n - 16.5). It holds when the multiplier of the zero is within 5 % of the linear prediction.
 Other predictions: every zero is unstable, word (01)^n, 2n alignments, no repeat; closure tier reliable (the closure scattered between 4e-10 and 1.6e-9 for n = 5 to 20, no trend); the scale free closest approach times |E| keeps falling slowly (0.4971 at n = 20; about 0.38 expected at n = 50 by extrapolating 0.497 -> 0.5015 -> 0.5063 steps of -0.005 shrinking); at large n one sense of the upper branch is stopped by a collapse of the step (as for n = 16 to 20) and the other reaches the zero in 250 to 600 steps.
 Table (n, theta, n T*_R, B with a + b/n, B with a + b n, multiplier linear, multiplier quadratic): predictions.txt in this folder.
